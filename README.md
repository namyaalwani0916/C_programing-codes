# C Programming - Unit 1: List of Programs

This repository contains a collection of fundamental C programs designed for beginners. The programs cover basic arithmetic operations, unit conversions, and geometric calculations.

## 📋 Contents

1. Add Two Numbers
2. Subtract Two Numbers
3. Multiply Two Numbers
4. Divide Two Numbers
5. Add, Subtract, Multiply and Divide Two Numbers
6. Convert Hours into Minutes
7. Convert Minutes into Hours
8. Convert Dollars into Rupees (1 $ = 48 Rs.)
9. Convert Rupees into Dollars (1 $ = 48 Rs.)
10. Convert Dollars into Pounds (1 $ = 48 Rs., 1 Pound = 70 Rs.)
11. Convert Grams into Kilograms
12. Convert Kilograms into Grams
13. Convert Bytes into KB, MB and GB
14. Convert Celsius into Fahrenheit: $F = (\frac{9}{5} \times C) + 32$
15. Convert Fahrenheit into Celsius: $C = \frac{5}{9} \times (F - 32)$
16. Calculate Simple Interest: $I = \frac{P \times R \times N}{100}$
17. Area and Perimeter of a Square: $A = L^2$, $P = 4L$
18. Area and Perimeter of a Rectangle: $A = L \times B$, $P = 2(L + B)$
19. Area of a Circle: $A = \frac{22}{7} \times R \times R$
20. Area of a Triangle: $A = \frac{H \times L}{2}$
21. Calculate Net Salary (Allowance = **10%**, Deduction = **3%** of Gross)
22. Calculate Net Sales (Net = Gross - **10%** Discount)
23. Total and Average of Three Subjects
24. Swap Two Values

---

## ⚠️ Important Note on `void main()`

> These programs use `void main()` to match the style taught in class. 
> 
> The standard-correct form is `int main(void)` with a `return 0;` at the end. Both compile and run; if a compiler ever objects, switching to the standard form is the fix.
